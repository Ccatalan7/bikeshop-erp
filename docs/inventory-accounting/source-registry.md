## Production facts captured on 2026-07-11

- Production had 1,655 products, 2,474 stock movements, 1,205 stock adjustments, 2,137 journals, 775 sales invoices, and 75 purchase invoices immediately before and after the inactive release.
- Product stock columns, posting-order ledger continuity, current-vs-latest-ledger balance, and journal balance all had zero drift after deployment.
- Twenty tracked products already had negative stock. The release prevents additional sales overselling but did not infer or rewrite those historical balances.
- The two product stock columns had zero current drift at inspection time.
- Sixty manual adjustments had no actor.
- No duplicate invoice journal headers or unbalanced journals were found by the initial aggregate checks.
- Product SKU `4715575883212` had current stock `6`, movement net `-10`, and 65 movement rows.
- Purchase invoice `208049` produced real reversal rows and phantom manual decreases at the same timestamps, proving the missing automatic-update guard.
- Production `handle_sales_invoice_change()` explicitly skipped stock for posted-to-posted edits.
- Repository trigger inspection exposes legacy job stock/journal writers, but their current handler returns before its deferred posting block. Live data confirms zero job-owned stock movements and zero job-owned revenue journals across 398 jobs; 396 are invoice-linked. The linked invoice is therefore the existing de facto posting owner.
- The July payment hardening is active: current production has zero fractional/non-positive/overpaid/exact-duplicate purchase payments and zero received-date/status drift.
- The historical payment fix was incomplete at the inventory boundary: 92 distinct phantom manual adjustments across 20 purchase invoices match purchase reversal timestamps. Invoice `208049` has four such rows.
- Of 71 active purchase payments, 69 still use legacy invoice-number journal linkage, one uses payment-ID linkage, and one (`FC-00015`, payment `c9eb167e-b19f-4f58-ac50-7ca596df8d8d`) has no recognized payment journal. Invoices `138518` and `AE010625` each have two duplicate legacy payment journals. The live handler safely converts only a unique amount-matched legacy journal and blocks ambiguous edits for audit review. No historical repair has been run.
- Production has 12 jobs with 2+ bicycles; all are currently fully paid and their job paid flags agree with invoice status. Their 67 invoice item rows include 25 historical rows without `job_bike_id`, so per-bike attribution is incomplete even though no invalid foreign bicycle IDs were found.
- Current linked-invoice reconciliation found 117 legacy product-level movement variances across 82 jobs. They are classified as `legacy_unresolved`; many involve historical service/product reclassification, while `FV-00809` includes the known posted-edit gap. No correction is inferred from this comparison.
- The movement audit read model has one row per raw movement (2,474/2,474 at deployment). For SKU `4715575883212`, it now shows the proven adjustment balances `9 → 0` and `10 → 0`, identifies their purchase-reversal collision, preserves raw quantities, and excludes only the exact duplicate technical footprint from summaries.
- Production drift had left `stock_movements.created_by` and `journal_entries.created_by` referencing the empty legacy `users_profiles` table. This blocked Quick Sale after actor tracing was activated. Migration `20260711190000` points both to `auth.users`; a rollback-only production smoke with the affected employee ID passed and business totals were unchanged.
