# Canonical UI Surface Registry

Website Builder work must also follow the mandatory parity, selection,
clipping, routing, media, and save contract in
[`website-editor-contract.md`](website-editor-contract.md).

**Purpose:** prevent business actions from being implemented on an unrouted,
obsolete, or partial UI while the screen employees actually use remains
unchanged.

## Required Rule

A business workflow is not UI-complete until the same shared action is present
on every registered surface where the underlying document can be operated.
Routes, list previews, embedded editors, side panels, quick actions, and mobile
layouts are separate surfaces even when they display the same record.

- Put business behavior in a shared service/widget; surfaces only compose it.
- Do not retain competing `*_old.dart` or unrouted detail/list pages. Git history
  is the archive.
- Before editing UI, trace the real route in `app_router.dart`, its barrel
  export, and every constructor reference with `rg`.
- After editing, verify the employee's normal click path, not only a direct URL
  or isolated widget test.
- Critical workflows require an architecture test that asserts every registered
  surface consumes the shared action and obsolete competitors stay absent.

## Visual and navigation continuity invariant

Every registered composition also follows
[`GUI_DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md`](../../.github/GUI_DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md); phone,
tablet, compact, adaptive, and responsive surfaces additionally follow
[`GUI_MOBILE_DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md`](../../.github/GUI_MOBILE_DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md).

A row in this registry documents one validated business host. Its table, list,
card, split pane, inline workspace, popover, sheet, modal, or routed
composition is specific to that task and viewport. It is not a template or
visual precedent for another module. Sharing one owner, command, permission,
and persistent effect does not require sharing one container.

When a registered surface opens related work, it preserves the exact origin as
applicable: module, query, filters, scope, tab, selection, disclosures, scroll,
pane state, and draft. Back, close, and cancel return there rather than to a
generic entity list.

Literal colors, radii, shadows, widget classes, and dimensions in historical
specifications are non-authoritative. Registry rows protect capabilities,
ownership, continuity, and validated task-specific composition; the two GUI
guides own current visual direction and how a surface is selected.

an action. The control remains on one line and never overlaps.

That last clause is a **contract with a shape**, not a wish (Design `5c`, the
decision-grammar board, reconciled with turn 7 `7a`). Every row falls into one
of five forms and a sixth is declared in Design before it is drawn:

1. **paid** — the chip *is* the button and opens the payment evidence;
2. **missing method** — tonal split with a caret, in `danger`, because nobody
   can be paid at all until it is set;
3. **transfer pending** — accent-filled primary;
4. **cash pending** — same rank, outlined, because the treasury differs;
5. **blocked** — **passive text, not a pill**: no fill, no border, no pointer
   cursor and no ink response, announced with `aria-disabled` and carrying the
   reason. A tonal pill here is the same figure as the four active forms, and
   with a draft week that turns the whole table into a promise it cannot keep.

The four active forms share one wrapper — 28 high, 186 max width, radius 8 —
so changing state never changes size or shape, and keyboard focus draws a 3 px
ring **outside** the border without replacing it.

**Nothing in this table may mount a `Tooltip` — not the decision cell, not the
disclosure caret.** A `Tooltip` is an `OverlayPortal`, and a *visible* one gets
reactivated by the reparenting a `LayoutBuilder` triggers on a band change,
mutating one `_RenderLayoutBuilder` from inside another's `performLayout`. It
crashes the module. Verified on a freshly started process with a real OS hover,
for both the decision cell and the caret; the caret's tooltip predates this
grammar, so the rule is the table's, not the cell's.

The blocked reason still has to be **seen**, not only announced: it is stated
once below the table with the canonical inline notice (`E-04 · VbNotice`),
which is what `7a` itself draws — "the foot strip sends you to Attendance". The
strip only speaks when every blocked row shares the reason; when they differ it
stays silent and each opened row carries its own, because one strip cannot
speak for two reasons without lying about one. On phone the same grammar holds;
only a reason that is about *that person* is repeated per card.
`PayrollRedesignPage` is the sole product tree; the deprecated
`PayrollListPage` compatibility entry delegates to it and may not preserve a
parallel visual workspace or its own behavioral test suite.

second message store.

the quick customer/supplier projections and `EntityChatSidebar`—delegates to
`MessagingService` and the atomic `create_staff_internal_conversation` or
`create_staff_support_conversation` command. The Flutter client cannot insert a
conversation or retained context row directly. Direct chats reuse only the
exact active two-staff graph; `conversations.is_group` keeps a one-invitee
group distinct. A customer support thread must resolve an active same-tenant
customer Auth account before commit, while supplier threads retain only
supplier/purchase capability. A locally durable operation key plus the server
receipt makes an uncertain acknowledgement replay-only.

The provider may outlive a route but never an identity boundary: auth user and
tenant form one explicit session scope. A scope change invalidates every list,
timeline, optimistic row, derived hint, view owner, timer, stream and prefetch
generation before the next scope starts. Results from an older epoch are
ignored, and a late REST prefetch merges monotonically without replacing a
newer Realtime timeline.

Operational removal is archival, never destructive deletion. Every employee
surface delegates to the tenant-scoped `archive_conversation` command, which
resolves the thread and records resolver/time evidence while preserving
messages, participants, contexts, provider receipts and linked workshop or
accounting history. Direct conversation deletion is not a client capability.

close-as-`preserved` and bounded cross-tab-residual wording in the
public-checkout row): the exit lease enters
`preparingOrder` before the first cart/session durability await. Losing that
lease clears only the matching receipt-free idempotency attempt and cannot call
the creation RPC; every pre-RPC failure with no creation attempt releases that
lease again. The initial snapshot is created only when the tenant has no active
attempt, and receipt attachment re-reads and extends the current durable
snapshot instead of overwriting it from a stale widget copy. The frozen
snapshot includes the exact opaque revision of the durable cart document; a
later same-SKU edit changes that revision and therefore preserves the entire
newer cart with an explicit warning instead of applying quantity arithmetic to
ambiguous units. Checkout and confirmation call one root single-flight in
`CheckoutSessionStore`: the standalone storefront owns that instance at the

critical storefront bundle, while the ERP shell retains its app-root instance.

tenant+order terminal tombstone, `applied` or `preserved`. A
confirmation remount waits for a live flight; without one, an orphan

`SharedPreferencesCartStore` propagates read/write/remove failures, verifies
platform writes through reload and read-back, and stores each tenant in its own
key. A matching legacy single-key basket is migrated once; a different tenant's
legacy basket is never deleted. Migration uses the same verified writer as an
ordinary cart commit. If an earlier migration left both matching copies, clear,
expiry and consumption remove the legacy residue first and the scoped copy
second, so a failed cleanup preserves the canonical copy and a later read
cannot remigrate purchased or cleared lines. Documents older than seven days or
beyond the allowed future skew are removed under that same storage lock on
read, including from a provider that remained open past expiry. `CartProvider`
distinguishes an empty durable
baseline from an unknown/loading/failed baseline: optimistic mutations remain
semantic pending commands until a successful read and catalog revalidation can
replay them over the recovered document. Both the standalone-store bootstrap
and the ERP-mounted public-store shell initialize that same tenant and catalog

A deeper unpublished category such as `Piñones` has no public label, card, click or route; a published descendant such as `Cassette` appears under its own name at the nearest surviving rail. An open overlay reprojects immediately after a publication refresh. |
| Mobile header navigation | Header menu button and recursive bottom sheet | `PublicStoreLayout._showMobileMenu` and `_buildMobileNavigationNode` | Mobile uses the same publication projection with mobile visibility flags. Menu-shell containers expand but never render `Ver todo`; unpublished intermediate category labels are absent, and every category offered as a tap target is published. |
| Desktop and mobile footer navigation | Footer columns and compact expansion sections | `PublicStoreLayout._buildFooterNavigationColumnsDesktop` and `_buildMobileFooterNavigationSections` | Footer rows pass through the same projection. A structural category is never emitted as a link; because the footer supports one link level, its published descendants are promoted into the owning section instead of being dropped or routed through the unpublished ancestor. |
| Category collection page and SEO eligibility | A projected menu destination, a sidebar category, a sitemap URL, or a direct `/productos/categoria/<slug>` link | `product_catalog_page.dart`, `projectStorefrontSeoRoute`, and the public sitemap generator | Sidebar tree, breadcrumb path, route resolution, indexability and sitemap eligibility gate on the same flag-derived publication set. A published category carried in a query string is upgraded in place (`replace`, never `push`) to the canonical collection path. Transient/legacy query routes remain `noindex,follow` with the clean collection as canonical. A durable route that resolves uniquely to a now-unpublished category is replaced with the matching catalog root instead of painting a customer warning; an unknown or ambiguous route still fails closed, is `noindex,follow`, and canonicalizes to that root rather than declaring the unavailable URL canonical. Product breadcrumb fallback keeps denormalized category text non-navigable until the authoritative trail proves current publication. |

Storefront CMS action-integrity addendum: public home blocks, dynamic CMS
pages, static policy pages, public Preview, standalone Canvas, composed
carousel Canvas and brand-logo links all consume the same
`PublicStoreLayout.isHrefPubliclyEligible` boundary before exposing a
navigable affordance. Contact-map actions and Team social links are part of
that boundary as well; secondary icons must not become a route around the
primary CTA contract. `WebsiteDestination.normalizeHref` classifies relative
links without a leading slash, `/tienda` mounts, trailing slashes, legacy
category query keys and absolute URLs whose origin belongs to the active
tenant as the same internal destination. Tenant-owned origins are resolved
once from the public URL, canonical legacy setting, current/custom/subdomain
hosts and the tenant detector's hosting aliases; apex/`www` and HTTP/HTTPS
variants share classification, while a foreign domain remains external.
Public rendering removes an ineligible category action rather than leaving a
clickable dead control or rewriting it to another collection; Edit retains the
authored control for repair, and the centralized click guard remains a second
defense. Brand artwork may remain visible after its invalid link is removed.
The destination audit consumes the same owned-origin registry and extracts
primary, `viewAllLink`, map and Team social destinations, so an alias cannot
masquerade as an unchecked external link or disappear from diagnostics.

The failure this section exists to prevent: the authored menus offered 107
category destinations while only 4 categories carried `show_on_website`. The
wrong fix — briefly shipped and reverted in July 2026 — was letting menu rows
act as a second publishing owner, which made "is this public?" depend on which
surface you asked. Public projection is the immediate safety boundary: it
narrows authored placement to the flag-owned set on every consumer. The owner
may later publish a legitimate category or remove a stale menu row, but no
data-reconciliation script may infer that a menu reference authorizes
publication. Menu/flag mismatches remain visible in the editor as diagnostics
until the owner resolves them.

## Asistente IA — resumen operativo (hoy / mañana)

Superficie: el panel del Asistente IA en el rail derecho (`ToolbarTool.aiAssistant`),
en escritorio como panel acoplado y bajo ~900 px como **reemplazo a ancho
completo del área de trabajo** —el mismo panel ocupando toda la pantalla, no una
ruta nueva—. No tiene entrada propia en el menú lateral y **no es una ruta**: se
abre desde el rail.

**Qué hace.** Ante «qué necesita atención hoy», «prioridades de hoy», «resumen
operativo», «organiza mañana» o «ayúdame a organizar el trabajo para mañana»,
responde con un resumen **determinístico**: entregas de Taller vencidas o
comprometidas para el día, y tareas pendientes vencidas, del día o urgentes sin
fecha. Máximo seis, con su motivo y su fuente.

**Qué no hace, y es contrato, no omisión:**

- **No pasa por Gemini** — ni para detectar la intención, ni para ordenar, ni
  para redactar. Un resumen sobre el que alguien planifica su día tiene que ser
  reproducible.
- **No escribe nada** y **no navega solo**. Ofrece tarjetas; el clic es del
  operador.
- **Sólo dos destinos**, ambos superficies agregadas ya registradas: Taller
  (`/taller/pegas`) y Tareas (`ToolbarTool.tasks`). Ningún editor, ningún
  identificador de registro, ninguna ruta nueva.
- **No dice «cero» sin haber leído.** Cada fuente informa éxito, vacío
  verificado o no disponible por separado; si una falla, el resumen se declara
  parcial y nombra la que faltó; si fallan las dos, dice que no pudo mirar. Y
  nunca afirma «el total del negocio»: dice cuántos registros revisó, porque la
  paginación de esos servicios no es explícita.
- **El día es el civil chileno**, vía `NotificationDigestWindow.businessToday`,
  también para cada fecha comprometida. Mañana se construye incrementando el
  campo de día, no sumando 24 horas.
- **`generatedAt` es el instante real de generación, en UTC**, y se muestra como
  fecha **y hora** en `America/Santiago` con el desfase vigente en ese instante.
  El sello acompaña incluso a la respuesta en que ninguna fuente respondió.
- **Ninguna excepción llega a la pantalla.** El motivo que se muestra sale de un
  conjunto cerrado (`AIAttentionUnavailableReason`); el detalle sólo va al log
  de depuración, y sin mensaje, tenant ni token.

**Aislamiento.** Cada fuente se verifica contra la autoridad del turno: el scope
que Tareas informa haber cargado y el de su servicio, el scope de Taller después
de la lectura, el `tenant_id` de cada fila y el de cada estado personalizado
adjunto. Una fila que no se puede probar invalida la fuente completa; nunca se
filtra en silencio.

- lectura y reglas — `lib/modules/ai_assistant/services/ai_attention_read_model.dart`
